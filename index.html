Who we are

About Al Furqan Online Academy

Al Furqan Online Academy is an online Qur'an learning platform created to make Qur'an education accessible from the comfort of home. We aim to help students build a stronger and more consistent relationship with the Qur'an through structured learning, guided practice and personalised lessons.

Our focus stays on the fundamentals: Qur'an reading, Tajweed, memorisation and basic Islamic duas, delivered through flexible online lessons that fit around each student's routine — whether that student is a child just starting out or an adult returning to the Qur'an after time away.
